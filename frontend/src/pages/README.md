# pages/
One file per screen:

- `ErrorScreen.tsx`: What went wrong, what to do, and buttons to upload another PDF or try again, and to go home.
- `Landing.tsx`: Home screen; title, the two buttons that pick the mode and open the file picker, and the fine print.
- `Loading.tsx`: Wait while the backend works: a DNA helix builds itself and, before the result, a percentage climbs.
- `Questionnaire.tsx`: Questions one per screen: body measures in both modes, habits in the full mode.
- `Result.tsx`: Biological age with its margin, the gap to the real age, the tables of years and share / download.
